I'm Harsh Ballyan, a creative professional based in Delhi, India, specialising in 2D animation, motion graphics, 3D animation support, and short-form reel content. With 4+ years of experience, I help brands turn ideas into engaging visual stories.

In addition to animation production, I shape reel concepts, edit pacing, and build branded visual systems that keep content sharp, engaging, and platform-ready.
